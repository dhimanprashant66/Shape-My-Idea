# Epic to Features, BRD & PRD

A reusable prompt for product managers who want to break a large epic or product idea into smaller features, define an MVP, and draft linked requirements documents.

## Quick start

1. [Open the portable prompt](./Epic_to_Features_BRD_PRD_Portable_Prompt.txt) and copy the workflow.
2. Paste it into ChatGPT, Claude, or your preferred AI chat.
3. Replace the placeholder under **My product idea or epic** with your idea and any known constraints. Send the prompt.

## What you'll get

- A feature breakdown with priorities, dependencies, and a proposed MVP.
- A **Business Requirements Document (BRD)** covering the business problem, scope, outcomes, and rules.
- A **Product Requirements Document (PRD)** covering product behavior, requirements, and acceptance criteria.
- A traceability matrix linking business requirements to features and acceptance criteria.

## Example input

> We want customers to report equipment faults and track their resolution. Customers need to see progress, and support agents need to triage incoming reports. We haven't agreed on response-time targets yet. Propose an MVP, then draft the BRD and PRD.

Review the drafts with your team and confirm any assumptions or open decisions before using them for delivery planning.
